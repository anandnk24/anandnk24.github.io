Anand Kannappan

I'm the co-founder and CEO of Patronus AI, a frontier data research lab steering progress toward human-aligned AGI. We develop world models to scale agent simulation data, which researchers use in model training.

Our mission is to enable scalable oversight - which I believe will be humanity's last research problem to solve. We are a series B company backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners, Notable Capital, Stanford University, and AI leaders across the labs.

Previously, I worked on AI at Meta, where I led early efforts around interpretability and causal inference. Before that, I ran a quant hedge fund backed by Mark Cuban and Howard Morgan.
